What you will know how to do

Results, not theory

Write a measurable goal — “from 3 out of 100 to 5 out of 100,” not “improve sales.” This is the first of the five answers that set up the loop.

Turn results into evidence — a tracking spreadsheet, with one row per proposal, support ticket, or post, which the loop reads each week.

Define what must never change on its own — the guardrails: what AI cannot touch and what must not get worse while the rest improves.

Decide with the five-line card — approve, reject, or wait for more data, knowing which sample size provides evidence and which only looks convincing.

Know what the loop does not guarantee — so you can trust what it does guarantee: no worse version goes live, everything is recorded, and you can roll back.

This course does not promise that your numbers will rise. It promises they will not fall because a system decided so, that every attempt will be recorded, and that you will know what evidence would make you discard your own idea.

Who this is for

Made for where you are now

this is for you if

  • You own a clinic, sell real estate, manage customer support, or run any business where the same task repeats every week — and already use AI to write proposals, replies, or posts.
  • You feel AI gets things done, but nothing improves: today’s proposal starts from scratch, just like the one from six months ago.
  • You use WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and an AI chat. You have never opened a black command screen and do not plan to.
  • You have 20 minutes per session and prefer to finish each lesson with something filled in.

this is not for you if

  • You want to learn to program agents or integrate systems — this course teaches the method and decision-making, not coding.
  • You want a prompt-writing course. Here, prompts are parts of a system, not the system itself.
  • You want a course with traditional video lessons.
